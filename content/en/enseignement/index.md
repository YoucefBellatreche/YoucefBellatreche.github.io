        ### University of Algiers 3 (since 2017)

        **Faculty of Economics, Management Sciences and Commercial Sciences**

        *Lectures*

        - **Electronic Project Management** – 2nd-year master's (lectures and tutorials).
        - **Introduction to Economics** (in French) – 2nd-year bachelor's: basic economic concepts of how markets work; market economy and free trade; monetary policy; macroeconomic equilibrium.
        - **Entrepreneurship and Business Creation** – 1st-year master's.
        - **Corporate Financial Policy** – 3rd-year bachelor's.

        *Tutorials*

        - **Quality Management** – 3rd-year bachelor's, management track: Ishikawa cause-and-effect diagram, Pareto chart (20/80 rule).
        - **Public Management** – 1st-year master's, management track: public management methods, public management in Algeria.
        - **Corporate Financial Policy** – 3rd-year bachelor's.

        **Faculty of Information and Communication Sciences (2019–2020)**

        - **Corporate Communication** (lectures and tutorials): internal and external communication, communication strategies, written and oral communication.

        ### Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University – Sorbonne School of Economics (2022–2024)

        - **Microeconomics: “Competitive equilibrium and market failures”** – perfectly competitive equilibrium in a pure exchange economy; perfectly competitive equilibrium in an economy with production; optimality and market failures.
        - **Public Finance and Taxation: “The economic role of the State and public administrations”** – why public intervention?; taxes and compulsory levies; changes in tax policy.
        - **International Monetary Macroeconomics** – 3rd-year bachelor's: balance of payments and capital flows; global imbalances and foreign exchange markets; interest rates and exchange rates; purchasing power parity, the Balassa–Samuelson effect, the Mundell–Fleming model.
        - **Methods** – 2nd-year bachelor's: essay with data analysis on “Youth employment and unemployment, 1982–2021”; working methods based on data processing and text analysis; building research questions, oral presentations, written syntheses.
        - **Introduction to Mathematical Modelling for Economics** – 1st-year bachelor's (semester 1): revisiting simple mathematical notions in a rewarding framework; an introduction to modelling; becoming familiar with the elements of modelled economics taught later in microeconomics and macroeconomics; learning to write, to explain and to make the most of a lecture.

        ### ENSSMAL, Algiers (2018–2020)

        Lectures, tutorials and seminars for fifth-year engineering students (Departments of Environment and Planning, and Living Resources):

        - **Project Cycle Management** (GCP)
        - **Introduction to Research Methodology** (IMR)
        - **Documentary Research** (RD)

        ### Teacher training

        Training in teaching practices and blended learning (University of Algiers 3, 2018) and in ICT for teaching (University of Constantine 1, 2018). Tools: Moodle, Opale, VUE.
