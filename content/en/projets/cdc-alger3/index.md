Since February 2025 I have been Head of the Career Development Centre of the University of Algiers 3, whose mission is training and career guidance for students. The annual programme is organised around three pillars:

1. **Career guidance**, based on a framework of the university's degrees (occupations, skills and prerequisites for each degree);
2. **Training**, through workshops for students;
3. **Events**: monthly debates and meetings with professionals.

From 2019 to 2022 I worked at the centre as a certified career guidance counsellor (World Learning Algeria), facilitator and trainer in job-search techniques.
