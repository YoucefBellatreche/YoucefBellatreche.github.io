In 2018 I coordinated the study unit set up by the Rector of the University of Algiers 3 to carry out a survey on the integration of the university's graduates into the world of work, within the TAWDIF project “from university to the world of work” led by the ILO in coordination with the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research.

**Target population:** the 2015, 2016 and 2017 bachelor's and master's cohorts.
