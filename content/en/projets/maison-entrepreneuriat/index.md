From January 2020 to January 2021 I directed the Entrepreneurship House (Maison de l'entrepreneuriat) of the University of Algiers 3:

- training sessions and experience-sharing round tables for students;
- entrepreneurship open days with public and private organisations;
- follow-up of students' investment projects in collaboration with banks and the National Agency for Supporting and Developing Entrepreneurship (ANADE).
