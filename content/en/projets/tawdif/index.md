From February 2018 to June 2021 I was the University of Algiers 3 focal point for the TAWDIF I and II project, implemented by the International Labour Organization (ILO) in partnership with the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research (MESRS). The project supports higher education and vocational training in Algeria in improving young people's employability.
