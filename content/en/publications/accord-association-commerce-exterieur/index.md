## Abstract

For Algeria, the implementation of the Association Agreement represented a very important step in the process of socio-economic and political development. This step was intended not only to increase and diversify trade between Algeria and the European Union, but also to boost the volume and value of exportable domestic production outside hydrocarbons. Indeed, Algeria must put an end to its economy’s dependence on hydrocarbons (oil and gas), as this is one of the key objectives of this partnership with the EU. However, the diversification of local production cannot take place without the strong involvement of productive foreign direct investment and without the “upgrading” of Algerian enterprises so that they become competitive – particularly small and medium-sized enterprises, which create jobs for individuals and financial resources (taxes and duties) for the State. Today, the objective of diversifying production seems out of reach after ten years (2005–2015) of implementation of the Association Agreement. Several internal and external obstacles prevent the achievement of this objective, which is essential for building a market economy.

**Keywords:** Foreign trade, association agreement, investment, development, trade.
