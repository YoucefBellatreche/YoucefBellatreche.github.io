## Abstract

The impact of digital technology on sustainable development is growing all the time, as innovations in new technologies lead to an abundance of data and massive use of computer hardware and equipment. It is an exponential growth of granular data available in real time. Contrary to popular belief, digital technology is not immaterial or virtual, but truly material. Its impact on the environment, caused by the manufacture and massive, perpetual use of digital equipment, continues to grow year on year. What is more, recycling technological equipment is only a partial solution.

**Keywords:** Digital technology, sustainable development, ecological footprint. JEL codes: Q32, Q56, O33.
