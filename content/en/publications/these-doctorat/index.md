## Abstract

The aim of this research, organized in 9 chapters distributed in 4 parts, is to understand why Algeria, despite its rich resources, does not manage to develop itself. This first presents an outline of the history of Algeria's economic development since its independence in July 1962, then it analyses Algeria's development process during the next three decades. Then, this research examines the impact of the partnership and cooperation agreements signed with the European Economic Community (EEC) and then with the European Union (EU) on the modernization of the economy, as well as the difficult and long transition towards a market economy since 1994. Lastly, this research shows that the economic policy adopted by Algerian leaders, mainly privatizations and socio-economic reforms, remain incomplete and inefficient, due to their ambiguous relationship with market reforms. They are thus unable to sustain the development of the country.

**Keywords:** Euro-Mediterranean partnership – free trade – trade openness – industrial strategy – development economics.
